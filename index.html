How the Smoothie Cup Works

Predict the World Cup. Build a smoothie. Drink your destiny.

1

The big idea

You predict what happens in the 2026 World Cup. Get a pick right and you earn a tasty 🍓 Yum ingredient. Get it wrong and you're stuck with a gross 🤢 Yuck one. At the very end, everyone blends their real-life ingredients into a smoothie… and drinks it. 😬

2

Sign up

Pick a username and a short PIN (4–8 digits). That's your login — no email needed. Your picks are saved automatically and you stay logged in between visits.

3

Make your picks, phase by phase

The tournament runs in 6 phases. Each one has a countdown timer showing how long until picks lock. Get them in before the clock hits zero!

⏰ Miss a phase's deadline and you're automatically handed that phase's 🤢 Yuck ingredient. Joining late? You'll also collect a Yuck for every phase that already closed before you signed up — so don't dawdle!

4

Two kinds of phases

Big rounds (Group Stage, Round of 32, Round of 16) are scored as a whole: hit the target number of correct picks and you win that phase's single 🍓 Yum — miss it and you get the 🤢 Yuck.

Knockout rounds (Quarter-Finals, Semis, Final) are scored per match: every match winner you nail adds a 🍓 Yum, every miss adds a 🤢 Yuck.

5

Lock it in

When you're happy, hit Lock In. Picks can't be changed after that (or once the deadline passes), so choose wisely!

6

Watch your glass fill up

As real matches finish, your My Smoothie glass fills with the ingredients you've earned, and progress bars show how each big round is tracking — live, no refresh needed.

7

Climb the leaderboard

The Leaderboard ranks everyone by how many 🍓 Yums they've collected. The more correct predictions, the tastier your smoothie — and the higher you climb.

🏆

The Grand Blend

When the final whistle blows, gather your ingredients in real life, blend them together, and drink up. Best predictor gets the tastiest cup. Worst gets… the celery. 🥬
